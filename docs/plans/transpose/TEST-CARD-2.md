# Transpose test card #2 · Settings

On your primary ASUS (data kept). Open ⋯ → settings.

1. **Find things.** Settings shows six topics with a one-line summary each. Open one, look, press Back.
   Can you guess where a setting lives before you open a page?
2. **Change things.** Toggle AUTO PiP off and on (PiP & BACKGROUND). Change FRAME RATE (SCREEN).
   Every on/chosen state shows a filled square, not only a color.
3. **Size.** SOUND & VIEW → SIZE shows ×. With AUTO on it moves closer/farther; with AUTO off it sets the size.

Tell Prime what still feels like a cluster.
